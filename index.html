    FAQ / Before you join

    Straight
    answers.

    Still unsure? Ask a question and we’ll reply.

    Which membership should I choose?

    Access if you train independently. Performance if you want coached group conditioning and a plan review every three months. Elite if you want two personal-training sessions built into every month.

    Is there a joining fee or minimum contract?

    No. The proposed policy is month-to-month with no joining fee. You pay the monthly price of your plan plus any add-ons you choose.

    How do I cancel or change plans?

    Cancel any time; it takes effect at the end of your current billing month. Upgrades apply immediately, downgrades from your next billing date.

    I’m new to training. Can I join?

    Yes. Every member starts with a 30-minute orientation, and we recommend the introductory assessment so a coach can set an honest starting point.

    Is personal training included?

    Only in Elite (2 × 60 minutes per month, no rollover). On Access and Performance it’s available as a paid add-on — single sessions or packages of 4 or 8.

    Can I visit before I commit?

    Yes — send an inquiry and choose “Book an introductory visit”. You can see the floor and meet a coach before choosing a plan.

    Are these prices final?

    No. FORGE is a design concept and these prices are illustrative placeholders that a real business would need to confirm before publication.
